Lima Super Power · 2-8-4

Berkshire
Division

Run a Lima-built Berkshire or a modern AC diesel over 13 miles of jointed rail: two long tangents across Ohio farm country, a grade over Kenton Hill, a through-truss river bridge, a working yard and a quarry branch. Every switch on the railroad can be thrown, and cars standing in the yard and on the spurs can be picked up and set out.
